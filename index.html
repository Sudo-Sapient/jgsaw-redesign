Layer 1

Core Context

Your business, documented once. A single source of truth.

  • Company profile, products, customer profiles, buying committee, competitors and brand voice in one place
  • JgSaw drafts it and you approve every part before anything uses it
  • Every deal, campaign and asset draws on it, so the whole team works from the same facts

Layer 2

Road to Win

Your winning habit, documented with evidence.

  • Every business has its own winning habit: how it finds, qualifies and closes the deals it wins
  • Captured in a working session with your key people, then backed by the evidence behind each point
  • Maps the customers you win with, where deals come from, what earns a reply, when you win, where you lose, where deals stall, what accelerates a deal and what proof moves a buyer
  • Every statement shows how strong its evidence is and traces back to its source. Where sources disagree, both are shown
  • Applied to every team member and every deal, and refined as deals close

Questions

What is JgSaw? +

JgSaw captures how your business wins, then applies tested sales and marketing methods to every deal, campaign and piece of content, building a winning habit across your whole business.

How is it different from using an AI assistant on my own? +

A general assistant starts from zero every time. JgSaw works from your approved Core Context and your evidence-backed Road to Win, applies proven frameworks such as MEDDPICC and E-E-A-T, and learns from every reply and deal outcome.

What do you need from us? +

A working session with your key people to capture your winning habit, plus access to the sources behind it. JgSaw drafts your Core Context and you approve every part before anything uses it.

How long does setup take? +

Talk to us. Timing depends on your team and sources. The fastest way to see JgSaw at work is to send us a deal: you get the output within one working day.

Does it connect to our CRM? +

Yes. JgSaw connects to the tools your team already uses, starting with Zoho CRM, so nobody changes how they work.

What happens when I send a deal? +

Name the company and what you'd sell them. Within one working day we send back what JgSaw makes of it: prospect research, buying committee mapping, competitive positioning, qualification scoring and a plan of next moves.

How is our data handled? +

Your context and intellectual property belong to you. Every statement traces back to its source, and you approve your Core Context before anything uses it. Full details are in our privacy and terms.
